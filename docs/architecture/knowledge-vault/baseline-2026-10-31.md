# Synthetic quality baseline — 2026-10-31

The 63 source-first fixture packs in `tests/quality/corpus.py` were run against disposable
PostgreSQL 17/pgvector at migration `0006_temporal_evidence`, with 200 unrelated synthetic
distractors. The search implementation under test is byte-identical to merged main at the start
of this work. Fixture SHA-256:
`9969247c78cf0063df6008586011639eb1d342d0b1a96d3c708be43fb131127a`.
The packs are template-derived and authored by one implementer; they have not undergone the
two-reviewer adjudication required for a trustworthy personal-knowledge quality estimate.

| Search path | Relevant cases | Recall@10 | Binary nDCG@10 | Empty result, 3 no-record cases | Temporal correctness | Search p95 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Existing strict text | 45 | 0.000 | 0.000 | 3/3 | 21/21 | 4.99 ms |
| Existing text + fake vectors | 45 | 0.078 | 0.029 | 0/3 | 21/21 | 15.39 ms |
| Experimental OR fallback | 45 | 0.889 | 0.801 | 0/3 | 21/21 | 13.55 ms |

The fake provider is a deterministic vector-pipeline diagnostic with no semantic meaning;
305/305 seeded assertions were indexed. It does not estimate production embedding quality.
The fallback was evaluated in the isolated worktree and **removed before delivery**. It
recovered relevant records on many synthetic questions, but every pure no-record question
returned unrelated candidates. A successful client abstention after seeing those candidates was
not measured. The 22 held-out packs share templates with development packs; their improved
retrieval does not establish generalization. Multilingual Recall@10 under the fallback was only
1/6. The production embedding model was unavailable locally and no weights were downloaded;
these text-only results cannot characterize the deployed hybrid search path. The timings are
local steady-state measurements, not deployed latency.

Three separate missing-model cases expect retrieval of an explicit record saying the model is
unknown; that record is relevant evidence and the answer should abstain. The other three
no-record cases have no relevant record and test candidate noise. Extraction coverage, grounded
answer correctness, citation correctness and client abstention remain **unmeasured** until
independent client judgments are supplied.
The fixed-seed case-bootstrap intervals were [0, 0] for strict-text Recall@10 and
[0.022, 0.144] for fake-vector Recall@10. They quantify variation among these templates only;
they do not support population claims. All reports were run with 41 development and 22 held-out
packs. The text-only index readiness was 0/305 by design; embeddings were disabled.

The strict text-only failure has a concrete mechanism: `websearch_to_tsquery('simple', query)`
requires all unquoted query terms, including question words often absent from assertions.
Neither this synthetic result nor the failed broad fallback justifies a new embedding model.
Before changing production ranking, gather independently reviewed private queries and evaluate
the client answer, especially abstention; then compare a guarded retrieval alternative against
this pinned corpus. Run commands and interpretation rules are in
[the quality runbook](quality-runbook.md).
